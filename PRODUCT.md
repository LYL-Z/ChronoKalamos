# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are Chinese-first visitors who want a bounded historical life in Chang'an, 742 CE, without being told that generated prose is a primary source. Secondary users include playtesters and, later, external historians reviewing provisional content.

## Product Purpose

ChronoKalamos is an evidence-bound AI historical life simulator. The first playable slice covers one city, one year, and three origins. Success is a roughly ten-minute session in which the visitor knows who they are, what they face, what a choice changes, and which passages are `史料记载`, `合理重建`, or `叙事虚构`.

## Positioning

The product mechanism is an authoritative save loop plus labeled historical evidence. DeepSeek may express a turn; it does not invent world facts or write the database. The map is a schematic evidence index, not a surveyed reconstruction of 742 Chang'an.

## Constraints

- Origins are fixed: western-market Sogdian merchant household, Chang'an craft apprentice, Jingzhao clerical household.
- Interface languages: zh, en, fr, el, ru. Historical content is only committed in reviewed Chinese, with English support. Other languages translate chrome only.
- Camera, microphone, payments, SMS, WeChat, QQ, and Passkeys stay disabled until consent, provider, and policy work exist.
- Guest saves must disclose browser-data loss. No implied cross-device recovery before authenticated accounts.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion` and the in-product low-motion toggle. No repeated flash effects.
- API keys, source prompts, and credentials must not enter client bundles.
- Brand commitment: Sand & Cinnabar. Sandpaper, cinnabar, indigo ink, and brass.

## Evidence

Product facts live in `docs/product-brief.md`, `docs/brand-and-ui.md`, `docs/upgrade-charter.md`, `docs/historical-method.md`, and `AGENTS.md`. Phase 11 public content remains provisional until external historian certification.

## Accessibility

WCAG A/AA is an existing release gate. Keyboard access, skip links, visible focus, reduced motion, text scaling, density, and high contrast are in-product. Pointer targets should remain operable at 390px without horizontal document overflow.
